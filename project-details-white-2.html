Dentaire - Dental Clinic & Healthcare Website

  • A modern dental clinic website designed to help patients explore dental services, learn about experienced dentists, and easily book appointments for their dental care.
Figma Design Figma Prototype

Project Overview

My Role: UI/UX Designer
Project Type: Website Design
Team Size: 1 Designer
Contribution: UX Research, Information Architecture, Wireframing, Responsive Web UI, Prototyping & Design System

Project Background

Finding reliable dental care and understanding available treatments can sometimes be difficult for patients. Important information about services, dentists, clinic facilities, and appointments is often scattered or unclear.

The objective of Dentaire was to create a modern and user-friendly dental clinic website where patients can easily explore dental services, learn about experienced dentists, understand available treatments, and book appointments. The website focuses on providing clear information, simple navigation, and a trustworthy healthcare experience.

Interface Design

Early wireframes focused on making dental information clear and appointment booking easy. The design aimed to help patients quickly explore services, learn about the clinic and dentists, and access important information without unnecessary navigation.

  • Clear access to dental services

  • Simple and intuitive navigation

  • Easy access to appointment booking

Final Design

The final website provides a clean and professional experience where patients can explore dental services, learn about the clinic and its dentists, understand available treatments, and easily book an appointment.

Key improvements included:

  • Clear Service Information

  • Stronger Trust & Credibility

  • Simplified Appointment Booking

Designing simple, meaningful digital experiences.
